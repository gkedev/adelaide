ChipIn

Let's find tennis balls that meet your tennis crew's demanding constraints!

Tell the recommendation agent what tennis balls you prefer. It searches real stores, keeps only what fits, and hands you a ranked shortlist. You decide whether to buy. Every step below ran live against Hellō, Keycard, Tennis Express, Holabird and Stripe's sandbox.

    Step 1

    The agent gets its identity

    ChipIn agent provider

    The shopping agent holds its own key. ChipIn issues it an agent token that names the player's person server, Hellō.

    ✓ aauth:chipin-shopper@chipin.aauth.tech · ps = person.hello.coop
  2. ✓
    Step 2

    Live catalog from real stores

    UCP

    With only its agent token, the agent searches Tennis Express and Holabird Sports over the Universal Commerce Protocol.

    ✓ 23 live offers from 2 stores
  3. ✓
    Step 3

    You sign in with Hellō

    Person server

    Hellō identifies you and links the agent to you. ChipIn sees a directed identifier, different at every shop, never your email.

    ✓ Person token issued · your shortlist is yours alone
  4. ✓
    Step 4

    Your constraints become a mission

    ChipIn

    ChipIn searches each brand you chose across both stores and keeps only the products that fit your packs and max cost.

    ✓ Mission proposed: Penn, Wilson · 12-pack, 24-pack · up to $100.00 · 12 of 87 live offers fit
  5. ✓
    Step 5

    Mission approved, four-party

    Hellō → ChipIn access server

    The agent asks Hellō for authority; Hellō federates the request to ChipIn's own access server, which checks it is your proposal.

    ✓ Auth token for mission:approve · federated from person.hello.coop
  6. ✓
    Step 6

    Keycard takes charge of your constraints

    Keycard

    ChipIn publishes your mission as a Cedar policy in Keycard. From now on Keycard decides what can be bought for you.

    permit (principal, action, resource) when {
  context.scopes.contains("chipin:selection:…") &&
  (context.scopes.contains("brand:penn") || context.scopes.contains("brand:wilson")) &&
  (context.scopes.contains("pack:12") || context.scopes.contains("pack:24")) &&
  context.scopes.contains("spend:within:usd:10000")
};
    ✓ Policy chipin-mission-… published in the Keycard zone

  9. ✓
    Step 9

    Purchase with a token

    Optional · your choice

    An authenticated person can complete the purchase. ChipIn's resource token names the exact order's hash, the auth token comes back naming the same order, and a Stripe shared payment token, capped at the total, pays for it once.

    ✓ Auth token for $89.95, bound to the order hash · next: a Stripe SPT capped at $89.95 pays it once

    This run stopped after authorization, so nothing was charged. The payment step itself is verified in Stripe's sandbox; no order is ever placed at the stores. Skip this step and the mission ends at the shortlist.
