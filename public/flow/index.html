ChipIn, Let's find tennis balls that meet your demanding constraints!

Tell the agent what you'll accept. It searches real stores, keeps only what fits, and hands you a ranked shortlist. You decide whether to buy. Every step below ran live against Hellō, Keycard, Tennis Express, Holabird and Stripe's sandbox.

    Step 1

    The agent gets its identity

    Chip In agent provider

    The shopping agent holds its own key. Chip In issues it an agent token that names the player's person server, Hellō.

    ✓ aauth:chipin-shopper@chipin.aauth.tech · ps = person.hello.coop
  2. ✓
    Step 2

    Live catalog from real stores

    UCP

    With only its agent token, the agent searches Tennis Express and Holabird Sports over the Universal Commerce Protocol.

    ✓ 23 live offers from 2 stores
  3. ✓
    Step 3

    You sign in with Hellō

    Person server

    Hellō identifies you and links the agent to you. Chip In sees a directed identifier, different at every shop, never your email.

    ✓ Person token issued · your shortlist is yours alone
  4. ✓
    Step 4

    Your constraints become a mission

    Chip In

    Chip In searches each brand you chose across both stores and keeps only the products that fit your packs and max cost.

    ✓ Mission proposed: Penn, Wilson · single can, 24-can case · up to $60.00
  5. ✓
    Step 5

    Mission approved, four-party

    Hellō → Chip In access server

    The agent asks Hellō for authority; Hellō federates the request to Chip In's own access server, which checks it is your proposal.

    ✓ Auth token for mission:approve · federated from person.hello.coop
  6. ✓
    Step 6

    Keycard takes charge of your constraints

    Keycard

    Chip In publishes your mission as a Cedar policy in Keycard. From now on Keycard decides what can be bought for you.

    permit (principal, action, resource) when {
  context.scopes.contains("chipin:selection:…") &&
  (context.scopes.contains("brand:penn") || context.scopes.contains("brand:wilson")) &&
  (context.scopes.contains("pack:can") || context.scopes.contains("pack:24")) &&
  context.scopes.contains("spend:within:usd:6000")
};
    ✓ Policy chipin-mission-… published in the Keycard zone

  9. ✓
    Step 9

    Purchase with a token

    Optional · your choice

    Liked the results? An authenticated person can buy any shortlisted item. Chip In's resource token names the exact order's hash, the auth token comes back naming the same order, Keycard re-checks the line, and a Stripe shared payment token, capped at the total, pays for it once.

    ✓ 8 × Penn Tour Extra Duty, $38.00 · Keycard ALLOW · auth token bound to the order hash · Stripe SPT capped at $38.00 · payment succeeded (sandbox)

    Sandbox only: no real money moves and no order is placed at the stores. Run without -buy and the mission ends at the shortlist.
