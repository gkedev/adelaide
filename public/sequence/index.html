Chip In: a shopping agent with a Keycard-enforced mission

The player picks pack sizes, brands, and a max cost. That choice becomes a selection mission across real UCP stores, approved by the player at their person server (Hellō) and enforced by Keycard as a Cedar policy. Every purchase is a per-call authorization: Chip In's resource token carries the exact order's hash, and the auth token is proof that order was approved. Payment is a Stripe shared payment token capped at that amount.

Trust grows tool by tool

catalog_search needs an agent token, the cart and mission_propose a person token, and mission_approve and checkout an auth token for exactly what is approved.

Offer and acceptance, both signed

Chip In's resource token carries the order's SHA-256 (the offer). The auth token carries the same scope after the player approved (the acceptance). Chip In charges only that order, once.

Keycard enforces the mission

Approving the selection publishes a Cedar policy in the Keycard zone. Every order line is evaluated by Keycard: brand, pack size, and the mission's total spending against the max cost.

The person exists apart from the agent

Hellō, not the agent, approves. The agent can't approve its own purchase; it only carries the proof.
